About BNBM PNG

Papua New Guinea's fast-growing Hardware, Home & Office supplier — part of the CNBM Group.

Introduction

Who we are

Specialising in wholesale and retail business in Papua New Guinea, BNBM PNG Limited employs more than 1,000 national staff around the country. From its humble beginnings and inception in 1992, BNBM has developed into a nationwide Hardware, Home & Office supplier with 13 Hardware and Home Centre stores in nine locations, and 11 Challengar Technologies digital stores.

BNBM PNG Limited comes under the CNBM Group, which is also the parent company to CNBM Material Co. Ltd and CNBM Investment Co. Ltd based in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, China. BNBM PNG Limited has established business operations across the provinces of Papua New Guinea, enabling network support in the country.

We offer excellent quality and reasonable prices, and ensure we meet our customers' demands at all levels — whether a D.I.Y. job at home, an office fit-out or a major construction project.

Our History

Three decades of growth across Papua New Guinea

  • 1992Pacific New Building Materials (PNBM) founded
  • 1994First Hardware branch established in Port Moresby, NCD
  • 1998Name changed from PNBM to BNBM PNG Limited
  • 1999Hardware branch established in Lae, Morobe Province
  • 2001Hardware branch established in Kokopo, East New Britain
  • 2002Home Centre branch established in Port Moresby
  • 2005Hardware branch established in Madang
  • 2010Integrated Hardware & Home Centre branch in Kokopo
  • 2012Home Centre branch in Lae; integrated branch in Kimbe, West New Britain
  • 2014Integrated Hardware & Home Centre branch in Rabaul
  • 2016Second Port Moresby Hardware branch opened at Varahe Street
  • 2023–24Expansion to Mt Hagen, Popondetta and Goroka; second Lae hardware store on Malaita Street; Challengar Technologies digital stores open nationwide
