Services & Delivery

Service centres, warranty support and delivery by road, air and sea.

Service Backup

Full backup Service Centres in every branch that stocks electrical and white goods. Our services are guaranteed under the warranty period for all products purchased from our outlets.

Delivery Service / Logistic Support

Every branch has a distribution centre for deliveries within city and town limits. We also organise deliveries by air and sea to the customer's tailored requirement — with minimal fuss.

Customer Service

We value customer satisfaction and take pride in sending customers away happy, every time. If we don't have the product, we make it our business to source it and supply.

How it works

Deliveries within town limits — and beyond

All our branches have a distribution centre for customers' goods deliveries within city and town limits, and we ensure all goods are packed and delivered in good condition. A reasonable land delivery fee applies. We also organise and ensure deliveries by air and sea according to the customer's tailored requirement, with minimal or no fuss at all.

For quotations on bulk supply, project orders or freight to other provinces, please contact your nearest branch — see Our Branches.
