BNBM Hardware

Everything for the build — from foundations to finishing, on one site, in every hardware branch.

BNBM Hardware stocks and sells all hardware products and materials for any building requirement, whether it is based on a D.I.Y. job at home, in the office, or a major construction project.

The range is organised into sections — Timbers & Doors, Cement, Ceramic & Vinyl Tiles, Electrical Tools, Steel & Roofing, Paint, Plumbing, and Plywood & Masonite. We offer excellent quality and reasonable prices and ensure we meet our customers' demands at all levels.
